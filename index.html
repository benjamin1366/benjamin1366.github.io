aura_rank

aura_rank is a personal tool that the owner uses to make and upload short videos to his own YouTube channels (@Aura_rank and Story Vault). It is not a public service and is only used by its owner.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channels, read their statistics and comments, and update the titles of the owner's own videos.
